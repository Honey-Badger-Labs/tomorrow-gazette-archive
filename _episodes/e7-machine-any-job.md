  - fact: Stanford's Canaries Dashboard, last updated 21 October 2026, tracks employment in a five-year balanced ADP payroll sample by how exposed each job is to AI. The dashboard measures a correlation with exposure, not proof that AI caused the change.

verdict_reason: Simon predicted that machines could do any work a person can do by 1980, not that they would take every job.
