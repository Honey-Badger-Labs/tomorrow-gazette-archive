  quote: "Before man reaches the moon, mail will be delivered within hours from New York to California, to Britain, to India or Australia by guided missiles."
  who: Arthur E. Summerfield, US Postmaster General
  when: 1959-06-08
  where: Remarks on the 1959 Regulus missile-mail flight, as quoted by the Smithsonian National Postal Museum

since:
  - fact: On 8 June 1959 the submarine USS Barbero fired a Regulus I missile carrying 3,000 letters. It landed 22 minutes later at Naval Auxiliary Air Station Mayport, Florida.
    sources: [S1, S2, S3]
  - fact: The Smithsonian National Postal Museum calls it the only time a missile was used to carry mail in the United States, and says missiles would never carry enough mail to make their use worthwhile.
    sources: [S1, S4]
  - fact: People reached the Moon on 20 July 1969, with Apollo 11.
    sources: [S5]
  - fact: In 2021 the US Air Force named Rocket Cargo its fourth Vanguard programme, studying rockets that could deliver up to 100 tons of cargo anywhere on the planet.
    sources: [S6, S7]
  - fact: On 20 October 2026 AFRL flew instrumented Rocket Cargo pods in a SpaceX Starfall reentry capsule as a test, and the Air Force recovered the pods after splashdown. It was a test, not an operational delivery or a postal service.
    sources: [S8, S9]
