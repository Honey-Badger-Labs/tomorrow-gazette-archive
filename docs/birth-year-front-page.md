# Your Birth-Year Front Page (draft)

`/birth-year/`: type a year and get a Tomorrow Gazette front page with a real, sourced prediction from that year (or the nearest one), what actually happened, and Dot's verdict. You can save or share it as a 1080 × 1350 or 1080 × 1920 card.

**Status: draft.** The page is not in the navigation, it sets `noindex`, and it is left out of the sitemap. Until the editor approves them, the proposed verdicts (102 of 107 today) carry "Draft verdict · awaiting the editor's OK". Setting `show_proposed: false` in `birth-year.html` shows only the 5 aired stories.

| File | Job |
|---|---|
| `birth-year.html` | The page (front matter: draft flags) |
| `_data/birth_year_predictions.json` | The public predictions (107 on 12 Nov 2026, one for 96 of the 111 years from 1916 to 2026, plus a few older extras), one place (generated by the prototype's `tools/build_data.py`, which re-checks every quote against the pages it was taken from) |
| `assets/js/birth-year/predictions.js` | Publishes that data to the page |
| `assets/js/birth-year/birth-year.js` | Year lookup, page, buttons |
| `assets/js/birth-year/card.js` | Draws the share cards on a canvas (same-origin fonts and masthead only) |
| `assets/css/birth-year.css` | Page styles, using the site's palette from `site.css` |

Verdicts for review: [birth-year-verdicts-for-review.md](birth-year-verdicts-for-review.md).

Typing a year shows that year's main entry (`primary`); other entries from the same year are one click away. A year with no entry shows the nearest year, with a note saying so.

Stories that haven't aired yet are not in this file at all. They are added on the first rebuild after their air date, so after each episode airs, rebuild the prototype data and copy it here again. Internal editor notes are stripped from this copy.

The page makes no third-party requests and loads no trackers.
