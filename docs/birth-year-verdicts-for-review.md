Every verdict marked ☐ is the newsroom's **suggestion**. None of these has aired. Until you OK them, the page and the cards print "Draft verdict · awaiting the editor's OK".

**102 proposed** (28 COMPLETELY BONKERS, 38 NAILED IT, 16 NOT YET, 20 PARTLY RIGHT), plus 5 already aired (unchanged). 16 more are held back for spoilers and are not listed here.

To change one: tell Grok which year and the new verdict, or edit `verdict`/`reason` for that entry in `tools/entries_1916_2026.py` (or `tools/build_data.py` for the older ones) and rebuild.
