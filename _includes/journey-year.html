{%- comment -%} Sets `year` from include.when. Most `prediction.when` values start with a year (`1940`, `1940-03-01`). A few are written as a month and year (`December 1900`); those would otherwise become "Dece" and sort as NaN. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign when_s = include.when | append: "" -%} {%- assign year_head = when_s | slice: 0, 4 -%} {%- assign year_head_n = year_head | plus: 0 -%} {%- if year_head_n >= 1000 -%} {%- assign year = year_head -%} {%- else -%} {%- assign year = when_s | slice: -4, 4 -%} {%- endif -%}
