# About the archive

## The show

[Tomorrow Gazette](https://www.youtube.com/@TomorrowGazetteAI) takes a real prediction from the past, such as flying cars, robot maids or electricity too cheap to meter, and checks what actually happened. Every episode ends with one of four verdicts:

- **NAILED IT**
- **PARTLY RIGHT**
- **NOT YET**
- **COMPLETELY BONKERS**

## Made with AI

Tomorrow Gazette is made with AI tools, and we say so. **Vera Tomorrow**, the presenter, and **DOT**, her floating fact-checking sidekick, are AI-generated characters, and so is their newsroom. The pictures of them on this site are AI-made stills from the show. Vera's voice is original and doesn't imitate any real announcer. The predictions and the facts are real, and every one is sourced.

## Sources

- Every prediction is real. Each episode page says who made it, when and where, with a link to a reliable source: an archive, a museum or a reputable publication.
- Every fact about the present is sourced too.
- If something can't be confirmed, we ask it as a question rather than state it as fact.

## Fiction is labelled

Anything imagined, such as Vera, DOT and the newsroom they work in, is fiction, and is marked as fiction wherever it appears here.

## Spotted a mistake?

[Open an issue](https://github.com/Honey-Badger-Labs/tomorrow-gazette-archive/issues) with a link to your source. We correct the page and note what changed.
